About

I like the messy middle of commerce, where the hard part isn't the screen but everything behind it.

For more than ten years I've designed products for payments, wholesale marketplaces, and retail. The interesting problems live in the systems underneath: payment terms, supplier relationships, inventory, and the teams who keep it all running.

Most of my work starts as an undefined opportunity. I help teams frame the business problem, make the vision tangible with prototypes, and turn it into a roadmap engineering can ship.

I sketch on paper first and increasingly build in code with AI tools, so ideas get tested in a real browser before they get debated in a deck.
