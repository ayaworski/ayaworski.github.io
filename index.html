About

I like the messy middle of commerce, where the hard part isn't the screen but everything behind it.

I've designed across B2C ecommerce, B2B SaaS, payments, marketplaces, and retail platforms. Working across different sides of a transaction has shown me where things break: in the handoffs between what customers expect, how the business makes money, and what operations teams can support. Those gaps are where I find the best product opportunities.

I carry that thinking from concept to delivery, turning ideas into tangible experiences. I design on canvas and build with AI, so an idea becomes something people can click and react to early.
