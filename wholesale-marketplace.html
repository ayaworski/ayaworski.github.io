B2BCommerceWholesaleMarketplace

Designing how brands and retailers grow their networks

How I turned a closed wholesale network into a discovery-driven marketplace MVP in five months, projected at $95M over five years.

By redefining the problem, aligning executive leadership, and evolving our design system in real time, I delivered a foundational experience to transform the B2B buying experience and accelerate network-driven growth.

The problem

Retailers juggled multiple brand portals to restock one store.

The legacy wholesale model was a closed network. Merchants could only buy from brands they already worked with, logging into separate brand portals and juggling multiple carts and spreadsheets to place orders. This siloed experience limited organic discovery, constrained new brand–merchant relationships and reduced the value of the B2B network.

Reframing the Problem

Wholesale runs on relationships, not shopping carts.

The initial ask was ambiguous: open up the wholesale network. During discovery, I noticed fellow designers framing the problem as a typical e-commerce shopping experience. But wholesale works differently: brands choose who they sell to, and pricing and terms are set relationship by relationship. I reframed the work toward a marketplace that supports existing brand–merchant relationships and makes it easy to establish new ones.

Discovery & Alignment

A vision leadership could get behind.

I led the team in rapidly prototyping the vision, then guided senior stakeholders through a story anchored to our ICP: discovering products across brands, initiating new brand relationships, comparing products and placing one confident order across multiple suppliers.

Executive AlignmentA visual strategy leadership could respond to and align around. It surfaced actionable feedback, secured leadership commitment and set a clear path to the MVP.
The solution

Connecting brands to buyers, and buyers to brands.

We shipped a marketplace that connects wholesale ordering directly to retail operations. Retailers can now discover and buy from brands beyond their existing relationships, and brands can reach new retailers.

Key decisionsCurated access over an open catalogBrands control who they sell to and at what price, so new relationships start with the brand’s approval, not an anonymous checkout.

Results

Shipped in five months, and built to keep growing.

We took the marketplace from concept to launch in five months, and it’s projected to generate $95M over five years. Lightspeed unveiled the multi-brand experience at NRF’s Big Show in January 2026 as a foundational step toward connecting wholesale, inventory, POS and e-commerce. The components built for it were later scaled across the Lightspeed platform, raising UX quality and consistency beyond the marketplace.
